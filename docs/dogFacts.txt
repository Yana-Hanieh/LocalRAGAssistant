# Dog Facts

Dogs are known to be humans' best friends. They have been domesticated for
thousands of years and are one of the most popular pets in the world.

Adult dogs have 42 teeth. Puppies, on the other hand, are born without teeth
and develop a set of 28 temporary teeth (also called deciduous or milk teeth)
by around 8 weeks of age. These baby teeth start falling out around 3-4
months old and are gradually replaced by the full set of 42 adult teeth by
the time a puppy is about 6-7 months old.

Dogs have a powerful sense of smell, with up to 300 million scent receptors
in their nose, compared to about 6 million in humans. This is what allows
dogs to detect scents that are far too faint for people to notice.

Dogs can also hear frequencies much higher than humans can. While humans can
typically hear up to around 20,000 Hz, dogs can hear sounds up to around
45,000-65,000 Hz, which is part of why they react to sounds humans cannot
detect at all.

Dogs do not sweat the way humans do. Instead of sweating across their skin,
dogs cool down mainly by panting and through sweat glands located in their
paw pads.

For comparison, an adult human has 32 teeth, while an adult dog has 42.
